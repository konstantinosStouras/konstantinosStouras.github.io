How we assess an answer

Reviewed by Codex on 13 October 2026. These are assistant-reviewed interpretations, not instructor-certified labels. The rubric accepts ordinary “ascending” and “increasing” phrasing as strict ordering unless the answer explicitly permits equality or adds a restriction. Ambiguous and non-answers are excluded from accuracy rates.

The grader first checks reviewed wording, then conservative rules for new paraphrases. It never uses a similar-looking phrase alone to assign correctness. Unknown wording needs clarification. Agreement on the reviewed examples is not an estimate of performance on unseen answers.
