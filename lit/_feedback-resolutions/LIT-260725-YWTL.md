Paper titles and affiliations no longer show stray trailing punctuation. Some publishers deposit a title with a dangling comma, semicolon or colon on the end — "The Lock-In Effect and the Corporate Payout Puzzle," — and the same artifact reached affiliation names ("University of Tokyo ,"). The Lit now trims these at ingest, both for new papers as they arrive and across the whole back-catalogue: 307 titles and about 14,000 affiliations were cleaned up. Titles that genuinely end in a question mark, exclamation mark or period are untouched, as is any punctuation inside a title. Related HTML-code artifacts ("R&amp;D", "&apos;", leftover markup) were fixed in the same sweep, so titles, abstracts and author names now display their real characters everywhere.
